### About

Melbourne-based data professional with a Master of Data Science from Monash. I turn raw data into insights that move business decisions  from SQL analysis and BI dashboards through to scalable pipelines and predictive models.

My focus is the analytics layer: performant SQL, dashboards that actually get used, and shaping data so the story is obvious. I back that with end-to-end data engineering and ML experience across the Modern Data Stack.

### What I do

- **Analytics & BI** — Translating business questions into SQL queries, dashboards, and reports stakeholders actually use.
- **Data Science** — Building predictive models and GenAI applications in Python.
- **Data Engineering** — ETL/ELT pipelines on Azure, Databricks, and dbt with Medallion Architecture.
